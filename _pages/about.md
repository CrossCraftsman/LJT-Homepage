I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 (2020-2024), where I was previously advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
